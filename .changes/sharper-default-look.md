Sharper default look. Palette: pure neutral greys, a deep ultramarine accent (`#1432c8`), and secondary text and status colours that all clear 4.5:1 as text in both themes. Structure: the three border tokens are now three roles (`--sf-color-border-strong` outlines a region, `--sf-color-border` is the edge of a control, `--sf-color-border-subtle` separates repeated things); the first two are ink in the light theme and reach at least 3:1 in both. A theme that overrode `--sf-color-border` alone must now set all three. Fields are ruled boxes on the page colour (`--sf-color-input-bg` equals `--sf-color-bg`), and focus doubles the edge in primary. Light-theme elevation is a hard cast at every level: a zero-blur offset of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6px in the frame's ink, with no blurred shadow; `Kbd` takes the same cast. `FieldLayout.Filler` loses its `dither` prop (a dither strip beside a field carried no meaning). `Box` loses its `texture` prop: the noise overlay was too faint to see, so remove the prop where it is passed. The decorative dot that led `Widget` and `ChatBlock` titles is gone. The `Chat` composer has no focus ring: it starts with a prompt sign (`>`) that marks the line as editable and takes the accent while the field has focus. Its send key is a one-unit square ghost key by default (`sendVariant` now defaults to `"ghost"`; the round machined key is gone, `"secondary"` and `"primary"` give a framed or a filled key). `Knob` stands toward the light by half of its wall plus cast, so the knob with its cast is centred on the scale. `Button`, `ToggleGroup`, `WindowArray` title bars and the `DataTable` / `Explorer` headers default to a flat face (`surface` / `headerSurface` opt back into a ramp), and table headers sit over an ink rule. Tinted fills behind coloured text are gone: `Chip` tones, `Calendar` events, `FormError` and the `Login` error draw their rim and label in the status colour on the page colour. `fonts.css` now also loads IBM Plex Sans (new optional dependency `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans`), which `--sf-font-sans` prefers. `ButtonGroup` and `ToggleGroup` keep their own width in a stretching parent.
