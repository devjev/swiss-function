Sharper default look. Palette: pure neutral greys, a deep ultramarine accent (`#1432c8`), and secondary text and status colours that all clear 4.5:1 as text in both themes. Structure: the three border tokens are now three roles (`--sf-color-border-strong` outlines a region, `--sf-color-border` is the edge of a control, `--sf-color-border-subtle` separates repeated things); the first two are ink in the light theme and reach at least 3:1 in both. A theme that overrode `--sf-color-border` alone must now set all three. Fields are ruled boxes on the page colour (`--sf-color-input-bg` equals `--sf-color-bg`), and focus doubles the edge in primary. Light-theme elevation is a hard cast (levels 1 and 2 a zero-blur offset, levels 3 to 5 add a blurred shadow). `Button`, `ToggleGroup`, `WindowArray` title bars and the `DataTable` / `Explorer` headers default to a flat face (`surface` / `headerSurface` opt back into a ramp), and table headers sit over an ink rule. Tinted fills behind coloured text are gone: `Chip` tones, `Calendar` events, `FormError` and the `Login` error draw their rim and label in the status colour on the page colour. `fonts.css` now also loads IBM Plex Sans (new optional dependency `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans`), which `--sf-font-sans` prefers. `ButtonGroup` and `ToggleGroup` keep their own width in a stretching parent.
