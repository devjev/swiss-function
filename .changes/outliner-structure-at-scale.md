Outliner: zoom, drag, virtualization and an imperative handle (issue #104, milestone 4). **Zoom:** a click on a bullet, Mod+. or Alt+Right zooms into a block, which becomes the outline's title with its children as the first level and a breadcrumb back (Mod+, / Alt+Left zoom out); new props `root`, `defaultRoot`, `onRootChange`, `zoomable`, `rootLabel`. **Drag:** a bullet drags its block, or the selected blocks, to the gap and depth the pointer picks, with a line that shows the place; `reorderable` (default true), dnd-kit loaded on demand, and the outline joins an `SfDndProvider`. **`virtualized`** renders only the rows on screen (give the outline a height). **`frame={false}`** drops the border, background and padding. **`apiRef`** (`OutlinerHandle`): `focus`, `select`, `edit(id, offset)`, `getSelection`, `undo`, `redo`. Each block now reports `aria-posinset` and `aria-setsize`, and the tree is named "Outline" when no label is given. **Changed behaviour:** a click on a bullet now zooms (`zoomable={false}` turns it off) and a bullet can be dragged (`reorderable={false}`); `role="tree"` and `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` moved from the root element to a child of it (the root is the frame and still takes the ref, `className` and the other attributes).
