`Kbd` gains `variant="outline"` (a flat ruled box per key that fits in a line of text) and reads a space in `combo` as a sequence (`"g u"`: G, then U, as two keys). `Field`'s `hotkey` now shows outline keys in the label row, at its end (after the label in the horizontal orientation); the raised keycaps no longer hang over the control's corner.
