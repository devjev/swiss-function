GridOverlay: a development overlay that draws the unit grid over a region and names its cells, for talking about layout with an agent. A cell is one unit square (it follows the density); rows and columns count from 1 at the top left, row first (`3:2`), and a range joins two cells (`3:2-5:10`). It draws the cell edges (stronger every `major` cells), `row:col` at the major crossings, the body text's baseline in every unit line (measured from the region's own face), and the cell under the pointer. It takes no pointer events, so the app works under it; the panel's Pick names a cell or a dragged range, copies the name and calls `onPick`, and `marks` outlines places by name, so an agent can show what it means. `cell` (1, 2 or 4 units), `major`, `layers`, `fixed` for the viewport. The naming helpers (`cellAt`, `rangeOf`, `formatRange`, `parseRange`, …) are exported. In the story server, `?grid` shows it over any story (`?grid=2` for 2u cells, `&mark=3:2-5:10`), and Alt+Shift+G toggles it. Closes #112.
