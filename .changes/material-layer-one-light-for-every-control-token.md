Material layer: one light for every control. `tokens.css` gains `--sf-light-x` / `-y` and the recipes derived from it (`--sf-edge`, `--sf-edge-soft`, `--sf-groove`, `--sf-cap-rest` / `--sf-cap-pressed` and their `-round` twins, `--sf-curve`, `--sf-engrave`), each with its own dark-theme value. Button, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Slider, Kbd and LaunchButton wear the edge bands and cap depths; Input, DigitInput, DigitInputMicro, Combobox (Picker / Selector), DatePicker and the Chat composer sit flush in a groove, with a cast only when `elevation` is passed. Pressed is travel (one step lower and 1px down), never an inverted dent. New `lib/surface` (`surfaceClass.dish` / `.dome` / `.flat` over `--sf-cap`) gives keys a face; Button, Kbd, Switch, Slider and LaunchButton take it as a `surface` prop (Button, Kbd and LaunchButton default to dish, Switch to dome, Slider to flat). Kbd and LaunchButton drop their private highlight tunables and dark overrides. The browser floor rises to relative colour syntax (`oklch(from ...)`). Constants from Ambient CSS's Blender-fitted model (MIT), rescaled to the 24px unit. ToggleGroup items are raised keys with the pressed one held down (`surface` prop, dish by default); DataTable and Explorer column headers are raised keys, the sorted column pressed. A `concave` face scoops a slot's floor: DigitInput cells by default, opt-in on Input and DigitInputMicro. The light comes from the top left by default, and the elevation and recess casts follow the vector (they fall to the lower right; the dark theme's hard cast already did). A `curve` prop (a multiple of `--sf-curve`, backed by `--sf-curve-scale`) sets the amplitude of a face per component or subtree. WindowArray title bars are raised keys (`surface` / `curve` on the array), sitting down while a window is dragged. TextEdit and TextEditInline sit in the groove at rest like every other slot. Stat.Group draws its dividers as a hairline ring per card, so an incomplete last row keeps its bottom and right lines. Tabs are folder tabs: raised cards on the sheet's edge, the selected one at full height in the panel colour and merged with it, the Indicator an index strip along its top (`surface` / `curve` on `Tabs.List`). A chromeless (`pip`) pop-out window gains a maximize toggle: a corner button from PopOut (`maximizable`), or in WindowArray's popped title bar (`usePopOutWindow()`), since the browser draws no chrome for it. VerticalForm anchors each rail label on its field caption's centre line (the row stays the density block), and Minimap decimates labels by the pill height instead of a whole unit, so the rail names what the screen shows; rail markers carry `data-kind`.
