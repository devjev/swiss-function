Outliner: typing assistance (issue #104, milestone 3). Brackets pair: `[`, `(`, `{` and a backtick insert their closer, a closer that is already next is stepped over, Backspace between an empty pair removes both, and with text selected those keys and `*`, `_`, `~`, `^`, `=` wrap it. Formatting chords: Mod+B, Mod+I, Mod+Shift+S toggle bold, italic and strikethrough, Mod+K makes a link. Tasks: a block that starts with `[ ] ` or `[x] ` shows a checkbox, and Mod+Enter cycles no task, open, done (in the text and on selected blocks). `#tags` and `#[[Two Word]]` tags render as wiki links. New `completions` prop: `[[`, `((`, `#` or `/` at the caret opens a list under the trigger, filled by the app (sync or async) and chosen with the arrows and Enter or Tab while the caret stays in the text; new types `OutlinerCompletion`, `OutlinerCompletionRequest`, `OutlinerCompletionTrigger`. **Changed behaviour:** Ctrl/Cmd+Enter in a block now toggles a task, and a `#word` in a block's text now renders as a link.
