Outliner: undo, block selection and clipboard (issue #104, milestone 2). One undo stack covers text and structure (Mod+Z, Mod+Shift+Z or Mod+Y): a step restores the tree and the caret or selection, and keystrokes in one block join into one step until a pause. Blocks select in runs: Shift+Up / Shift+Down from a block's first / last line select it and then extend block by block, Mod+A selects the text and then every block, and a drag from one block into another or a Shift+click selects the blocks between. Tab, the move chords and Backspace / Delete act on every selected block. Copy and cut put the selected blocks on the clipboard as a markdown list; pasted lists and blank-line-separated paragraphs become nested blocks, while a single line still pastes as text. **Changed behaviour:** a selected block includes its children (they are tinted with it), and deleting a selected block now removes its children too (they moved up a level before); undo brings them back. The root is `aria-multiselectable`.
